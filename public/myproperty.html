1 Who is listing this?

Every listing must carry a valid registration number issued by the Board of Valuers, Appraisers, Estate Agents and Property Managers (LPPEH/BOVAEA). This is the trust layer Malaysia's market is missing — REINS enforces the same thing in Japan through mandatory registration.

Format: REN + 5 digits, or E(3)1234 for agencies.
